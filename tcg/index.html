What can you do in FP TCG?

Everything you'd expect from a card game, made with FootParadise art.

Open packs

Get a welcome pack when you sign up and a free pack every 9 hours. Watch the opening animation and see what you pull. Pack odds are shown inside the game.

Complete the album

300 cards from Dragon Ball, Naruto, One Piece, Jujutsu Kaisen, Demon Slayer, Hunter x Hunter, Genshin Impact and many more. Tilt them in 3D and watch the Holo and Gold effects.

PVP battles

Build a deck of 20 characters + 30 action cards (attacks, magic, traps and fields). Knock out rivals to score points: the first to 20 points wins. Practice against the CPU first!

Ranked league

Climb from Bronze to Legend in 90-day seasons and earn medals. You can also duel your friends whenever you want.

Trade with players

Ask for the cards you're missing, make offers in the market and swap duplicates with other players and friends.

Minigames

3D claw machine, gashapon, roulette, plinko and memory to win cards, tickets and dust. Plus daily streaks, missions, achievements and a collector pass.

Make it yours

Avatars, profile frames, playmats and card backs. Unlock them by playing or get them in the in-game shop.

Craft cards

Turn your duplicates into FP Dust and craft the cards you need to finish your collection.

How to play

Choose the version for your device. It's the same game and the same account in both.

Web version

For iPhone, iPad, PC and Mac. Nothing to install.

  1. Open tcg.footparadiseart.com in your browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge…).
  2. Choose your language and confirm you are 18 or older.
  3. Create your account with your email and a password, then confirm it from the email we send you.
  4. Open your welcome pack and follow the tutorial. Have fun!
On a computer the game shows inside a phone-shaped frame. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to open it like an app.

Android app (APK)

For Android phones and tablets. The smoothest way to play.

  1. Tap Download APK. MediaFire opens: tap its blue DOWNLOAD button (ignore any ads or pop-ups).
  2. When it finishes, open the file FootParadiseTCG-v1.0.12.apk from your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager, go back and tap Install.
  4. Open FootParadise TCG, create your account and open your welcome pack.
You only download it once: when there's a new version, the app tells you and updates itself.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You can play, open free packs, battle and trade without paying. Extra packs and FP$ are optional and are bought through Payhip.

I have an iPhone. Can I play?

Yes, with the web version: open tcg.footparadiseart.com in Safari. The APK only works on Android.

Do I have to download the APK again for each update?

No. When there's a new version, the app shows it and installs the update by itself. The web version updates when you reload the page.

Can I use the same account on the web and in the app?

Yes. Your cards, decks and progress are saved in your account, so you can switch between devices whenever you want.

Android says the app may be unsafe. Is that normal?

Yes. It's the usual warning for apps installed outside the Play Store. Download it only from the official button on this page.

Do I need internet?

Yes. Packs, trades and battles happen online, so you need a connection to play.
